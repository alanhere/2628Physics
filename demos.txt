date: 2026-10-06
title: Van de Graaff generator
video: Hair standing on end | https://www.youtube.com/
notice: The volunteer's hair spreads out and rises. Sparks jump to the metal dome when a rod is brought close.
physics: The belt carries charge up to the dome. Charges of the same sign repel, so each hair pushes away from its neighbours.
physics: A spark happens when the air can no longer insulate the gap.

date: 2026-10-01
title: Hammer and feather in a vacuum
video: Falling with and without air | https://www.youtube.com/
notice: In air the feather drifts down. With the air pumped out, both hit the bottom at the same moment.
physics: Gravity accelerates every object at the same rate. Air resistance is the only reason the feather falls slowly.

date: 2026-09-25
title: Slinky waves
video: Pushing and shaking a spring | https://www.youtube.com/
notice: Push along the spring and a pulse of squashed coils travels down it. Shake it sideways and a different kind of wave appears.
physics: Pushing along the spring makes a longitudinal wave. Shaking it sideways makes a transverse wave. In both, the energy travels but each coil only moves back and forth.

date: 2026-09-18
title: Swinging bowling ball
video: Will it hit? | https://www.youtube.com/
notice: The ball is released from the tip of the nose, swings away and comes back, stopping just short.
physics: Gravitational potential energy turns into kinetic energy and back again. Some energy is always lost to air resistance, so the ball never returns higher than it started.
