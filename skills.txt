date: 2026-09-08
title: Rearranging equations
topic: Equations
about: Making a different quantity the subject of an equation.
explain: Whatever you do to one side of an equation, you must do to the other. Work in small steps and undo operations in the reverse order.
example: Make a the subject of v = u + at.
example: Subtract u from both sides: v - u = at
example: Divide both sides by t: a = (v - u) / t

title: Reading a gradient
topic: Graphs
about: Finding the gradient of a straight line and saying what it means.
