date: 2026-10-02
title: Stretching a spring
aim: To find how the extension of a spring depends on the force pulling it.
equipment: Spring; Clamp stand and clamp; Metre rule; Slotted masses, 100 g each; Paper-clip pointer
method:
- Hang the spring from the clamp and measure its length with no mass.
- Add one 100 g mass (a force of 1 N) and measure the new length.
- Add a further mass and repeat until you reach 5 N.
- Subtract the original length from each reading to get the extension.
photos: sample-setup.jpg
table: Extension (cm) | Force (N)
row: 0 | 0
row: 1.9 | 1
row: 4.1 | 2
row: 6.0 | 3
row: 8.2 | 4
row: 10.1 | 5
graph: yes
fit: origin
conclusion: Force is directly proportional to extension up to 10 cm. The gradient gives a spring constant of about 50 N/m.

date: 2026-09-26
title: Trolley down a ramp
aim: To measure how the acceleration of a trolley changes with the height of the ramp.
equipment: Trolley; Ramp and books; Light gate and timer; Metre rule
method:
- Set the ramp to a height of 5 cm.
- Release the trolley from the same mark each time.
- Record the acceleration from the light gate.
- Raise the ramp by 5 cm and repeat.
table: Ramp height (cm) | Acceleration (m/s²)
row: 5 | 0.4
row: 10 | 0.9
row: 15 | 1.3
row: 20 | 1.8
graph: yes
fit: line
conclusion: Acceleration increases with the height of the ramp. Doubling the height roughly doubles the acceleration.

date: 2026-09-12
title: Heating an aluminium block
aim: To find the specific heat capacity of aluminium.
equipment: 1 kg aluminium block with two holes; 12 V heater and thermometer; Joulemeter; Insulating cloth
method:
- Place the heater and thermometer in the block and wrap it in cloth.
- Record the starting temperature and joulemeter reading.
- Switch on the heater and record every 100 seconds.
- Plot energy supplied against temperature rise.
table: Time (s) | Temperature (°C)
row: 0 | 20
row: 100 | 25
row: 200 | 30
row: 300 | 36
row: 400 | 41
conclusion: The temperature rose steadily. Heat lost to the air means the measured value is slightly too high.
