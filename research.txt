date: 2026-10-04
title: Why is the sky blue?
question: Why does the sky look blue in the day but red at sunset?
found: Sunlight contains every colour. Air molecules scatter short wavelengths, such as blue, much more than long wavelengths, such as red.
found: At sunset the light travels through more air. Most of the blue is scattered out of the beam, leaving the reds and oranges.

date: 2026-09-28
title: How does a wind turbine make electricity?
question: How is the movement of the wind turned into electrical energy?
found: Moving air turns the blades, which turn a shaft connected to a generator.
found: Inside the generator, magnets spin past coils of wire. This changes the magnetic field through the coils and induces a voltage.
sources: Sample source 1: energy company guide | https://example.com/source1
further: Find out why turbine blades are longer on offshore wind farms.

date: 2026-09-14
title: How do seat belts reduce injury?
question: Why does wearing a seat belt make a crash less harmful?
found: In a crash, a passenger keeps moving at the car's original speed until a force stops them.
found: A seat belt stretches slightly, which makes the stopping time longer. A longer time means a smaller force, because force equals the change in momentum divided by time.
sources: Sample source 1: road safety website | https://example.com/source1
further: Look up how crumple zones use the same idea.
